# 48 · Salmon Poke Bowl

**Cuisine:** Hawaiian-Japanese · **Method:** No cook · **Total time:** 12 min (12 prep / 0 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> A four-ingredient marinade, ten minutes of chopping, and fish that never meets heat at any point.

## Why it works
Because nothing is cooked, the salmon must be sashimi-grade — sold specifically for eating raw, and frozen beforehand to kill parasites. Cutting it into 2 cm cubes rather than thin slices gives the soy and sesame more surface to cling to while leaving a centre that stays silky instead of curing firm.

## Ingredients

**For the fish**
- 500 g (1 lb 2 oz) sashimi-grade salmon, skinned, in 2 cm (¾ in) cubes
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) soy sauce or tamari
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) rice vinegar
- 1 tsp honey
- 2 tsp finely grated fresh ginger
- 4 spring onions, finely sliced

**For the bowls**
- ½ small white cabbage, finely shredded
- 1 cucumber, in half-moons
- 2 ripe avocados, sliced
- 6 radishes, thinly sliced
- 1 sheet nori, snipped into strips
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Whisk the soy, sesame oil, vinegar, honey and ginger together in a mixing bowl.
2. Add the salmon and half the spring onions and turn them gently through the marinade. Leave 10 minutes while you build the bowls — past 20 the surface goes pale and firm.
3. Shred the cabbage and slice the cucumber, avocado and radishes on one board. Divide between four bowls.
4. Spoon the salmon and all its marinade over the top.
5. Finish with nori strips, sesame seeds, the rest of the spring onion and a lime wedge each.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** No sashimi-grade fish, no problem. Use 400 g hot-smoked salmon or smoked trout, flaked into large pieces, or cold poached salmon. Halve the soy for either, as both are already salted.
- **Make it faster:** Ask the fishmonger to skin the fillet and you save three minutes and a sharp knife.
- **Bulk it out:** 250 g cooked and cooled sushi or brown rice under the salad makes it a proper poke bowl.
- **Leftovers:** Eat the same day. Raw marinated fish does not keep.
