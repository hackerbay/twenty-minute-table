# 46 · Deconstructed Vietnamese Summer Roll Bowl

**Cuisine:** Vietnamese · **Method:** No cook · **Total time:** 15 min (15 prep / 0 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Everything that goes inside a summer roll, without the wrapper wrestling. The kettle is the only heat.

## Why it works
Fine rice vermicelli is thin enough to soften right through in off-the-boil kettle water in six minutes, so the hob stays cold. Nuoc cham is a ratio rather than a recipe — equal parts fish sauce, lime and water, half as much honey — so you can taste and correct it in the bowl you serve from.

## Ingredients

**For the bowls**
- 200 g (7 oz) fine rice vermicelli
- 400 g (14 oz) firm tofu, drained and cut into 1 cm (½ in) slabs
- 1 cucumber, halved and cut into matchsticks
- 2 carrots, coarsely grated
- 1 baby gem lettuce, shredded
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) mint leaves
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) coriander
- a handful of Thai basil, if you can find it
- 60 g (½ cup) roasted peanuts, roughly crushed

**For the nuoc cham**
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) fish sauce
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) lime juice, about 2 limes
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) warm water
- 1½ tbsp honey
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 red chilli, finely sliced

## Method
1. Boil the kettle. Put the vermicelli in a large bowl, cover with hot water by 2 cm (¾ in) and leave 6 minutes.
2. Meanwhile whisk the nuoc cham in the serving bowl until the honey dissolves. Taste: sharp first, salty second, barely sweet.
3. Cut the vegetables and pick the herbs on one board, leaves whole so they stay springy.
4. Drain the noodles and rinse under the cold tap until cool and no longer tacky — this is what stops them setting into a brick.
5. Turn the tofu in 2 tbsp of the dressing and leave 2 minutes; it takes the liquid up like a sponge.
6. Divide the noodles between four bowls, pile on vegetables, herbs and tofu, spoon over the rest of the dressing, finish with peanuts.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** 300 g cooked cold king prawns or shredded poached chicken in place of the tofu.
- **Make it faster:** Put carrot and cucumber through the same box grater and skip the knife.
- **Bulk it out:** A second block of tofu, or half an avocado per person.
- **Leftovers:** Keep the dressing separate and it holds 2 days. Dressed noodles go soft overnight.
