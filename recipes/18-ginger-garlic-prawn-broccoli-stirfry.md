# 18 · Ginger-Garlic Prawn & Broccoli Stir-Fry

**Cuisine:** Chinese · **Method:** Wok · **Total time:** 10 min (5 prep / 5 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> A splash of water and a lid turn the wok into a steamer, so the broccoli is done before the prawns go in.

## Why it works
Stir-frying alone scorches broccoli before the stems soften, so a covered steam does the interior and open heat does the browning. Mixing the cornflour into the sauce in advance means it thickens in seconds — long enough to glaze, too quick to overcook the prawns.

## Ingredients
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) raw king prawns, peeled and patted dry
- 400 g (14 oz) broccoli, in small florets, stem peeled and sliced
- 30 g (1 oz) ginger, finely julienned
- 5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) groundnut or vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) water, plus 60 ml (¼ cup) for steaming
- 2½ tbsp (37 ml) light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) Shaoxing wine or dry sherry
- 2 tsp rice vinegar
- 2 tsp honey
- 2 tsp cornflour (cornstarch)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 3 spring onions, in 3 cm lengths

## Method
1. Stir the soy, Shaoxing wine, vinegar, honey, cornflour, sesame oil, chilli flakes and 3 tbsp water in a small bowl until smooth.
2. Heat the wok until a drop of water skitters and vanishes. Add 1 tbsp oil, then the broccoli, and toss 1 minute for colour.
3. Pour in the 60 ml water, cover and steam 90 seconds. Lift the lid — the water should be gone, the florets bright green with give in the stem. Tip onto a plate.
4. Back on high heat with the remaining oil. Add the ginger and garlic, stir 20 seconds, then the prawns in one layer. Leave 45 seconds, then toss 1 minute until opaque and just curling.
5. Return the broccoli, stir the sauce and pour it in. Toss 30–45 seconds until glossy and clinging. Add the spring onions, toss once and serve.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Sliced chicken breast needs 3 minutes instead of 2. Tenderstem or green beans swap straight in; mangetout skips the steam.
- **Make it faster:** Buy the broccoli ready-cut and this is a five-minute dinner.
- **Bulk it out:** Rice, or 300 g cooked egg noodles added with the sauce to take on the glaze.
- **Leftovers:** Keeps 2 days. Eat cold or reheat fast in the wok; a microwave overcooks the prawns.
