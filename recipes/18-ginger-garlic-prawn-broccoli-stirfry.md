# 18 · Ginger-Garlic Prawn & Broccoli Stir-Fry

**Cuisine:** Chinese · **Method:** Wok · **Total time:** 15 min (7 prep / 8 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> A splash of water and a lid turn the wok into a steamer, so the broccoli is done before the prawns go in.

## Why it works
Stir-frying alone scorches broccoli before the stems soften, so a covered steam does the interior and open heat does the browning. Mixing the cornflour into the sauce in advance means it thickens in seconds — long enough to glaze, too quick to overcook the prawns. The noodles soften in a bowl of boiling water while the wok runs and go in at the end, where they take on the sauce that would otherwise sit in the bottom of the pan.

## Ingredients

**For the noodles**
- 300 g (10 oz) dried medium egg noodles

**For the wok**
- 700 g (1 lb 9 oz) raw king prawns, peeled and patted dry
- 450 g (1 lb) broccoli, in small florets, stem peeled and sliced
- 200 g (7 oz) sugar snap peas or mangetout
- 1 red pepper (~150 g / 5 oz), sliced
- 30 g (1 oz) ginger, finely julienned
- 5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) groundnut or vegetable oil
- 60 ml (¼ cup) water, for steaming
- 3 spring onions, in 3 cm lengths
- 60 g (½ cup) roasted cashews, roughly chopped

**For the sauce**
- 2½ tbsp (37 ml) light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) Shaoxing wine or dry sherry
- 2 tsp rice vinegar
- 2 tsp honey
- 2 tsp cornflour (cornstarch)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) water

## Method
1. Boil the kettle. Put the noodles in a large heatproof bowl, cover with boiling water and leave 5 minutes, until loose and just short of soft, then drain in a colander and shake dry.
2. Stir the soy, Shaoxing wine, vinegar, honey, cornflour, sesame oil, chilli flakes and 3 tbsp water in a small bowl until smooth.
3. Heat the wok until a drop of water skitters and vanishes. Add 1 tbsp oil, then the broccoli and pepper, and toss 1 minute for colour.
4. Pour in the 60 ml water, add the sugar snaps, cover and steam 90 seconds. Lift the lid — the water should be gone, the florets bright green with give in the stem. Tip onto a plate.
5. Back on high heat with the remaining oil. Add the ginger and garlic, stir 20 seconds, then the prawns in one layer. Leave 45 seconds, then toss 1 minute until opaque and just curling.
6. Return the vegetables with the drained noodles, stir the sauce and pour it in. Toss 1 minute until everything is glossy and clinging; if the wok is too crowded to move, tip it all back into the noodle bowl and toss there. Add the spring onions and cashews, toss once and serve.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Sliced chicken breast needs 3 minutes instead of 2. Tenderstem or green beans swap straight in; mangetout skips the steam.
- **Make it faster:** Buy the broccoli ready-cut and use straight-to-wok noodles — the wok work is still five minutes.
- **On the side:** A jar of chilli crisp on the table, and cucumber smacked with the flat of a knife and dressed with black vinegar and garlic.
- **Leftovers:** Keeps 2 days. Eat cold or reheat fast in the wok; a microwave overcooks the prawns.
