# 07 · Chilli-Lime Prawns with Avocado & Coriander

**Cuisine:** Mexican · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 10 min (4 prep / 6 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Six minutes from cold prawns to hot, smoky, lime-sharp ones, tipped over avocado that has been mashed roughly on the board.

## Why it works
Prawns cook by residual heat as much as anything, so they go in dry and come out at the moment they curl into a loose C — a tight O means they have gone one minute too far. The lime juice is added off the heat: in the basket it would boil away, but poured over hot prawns it lifts steam and carries the chilli with it.

## Ingredients

**For the basket**
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) raw king prawns, peeled and deveined, patted dry
- 1½ tbsp (22 ml) olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried chilli flakes, or 1 red chilli finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- ½ tsp fine sea salt

**To finish**
- 2 large ripe avocados
- Juice of 2 limes, plus 1 more cut into wedges
- ½ small red onion, very finely sliced
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) coriander, leaves and stalks, chopped

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Toss the dry prawns in a bowl with the oil, paprika, cumin, chilli, garlic and salt until they are evenly rust-coloured.
2. Spread them in a single layer in the basket — crowding them steams them grey rather than roasting them.
3. Air fry for 6 minutes, shaking once at 4 minutes. They are ready the moment they turn opaque pink and curl into a loose C shape.
4. While they cook, halve and stone the avocados, scoop the flesh onto the board and crush it roughly with a fork, leaving lumps. Spread across a platter or four plates and season.
5. Tip the prawns and everything clinging to the basket over the avocado, squeeze the lime juice on while they still hiss, then scatter with red onion and coriander. Serve with lime wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Scallops need 5 minutes, firm white fish chunks 7. Frozen prawns work if defrosted and properly dried.
- **Make it faster:** Mix the spices with the oil in the bowl the night before and the prawns need 30 seconds of tossing.
- **Bulk it out:** Warm corn tortillas, or black beans heated through with a little of their own liquid.
- **Leftovers:** Eat within a day, cold, in a tortilla — reheating turns prawns rubbery.
