# 06 · Za'atar Chicken with Lemon Courgette

**Cuisine:** Levantine · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 18 min (5 prep / 13 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Za'atar toasts on the surface of the chicken while courgettes underneath collapse into something halfway between roasted and braised.

## Why it works
Za'atar is mostly sesame and dried thyme, and both scorch quickly, so the blend goes on under a film of olive oil that slows the heat and lets it toast rather than burn. Butterflying the breasts to an even thickness gets them cooked in thirteen minutes, exactly as long as thick courgette half-moons need to give up their water and brown.

## Ingredients

**For the basket**
- 4 chicken breasts (~200 g / 7 oz each), butterflied and opened flat
- 3 courgettes (~600 g / 1 lb 5 oz), cut into 2 cm (¾ in) half-moons
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) olive oil
- 3 tbsp za'atar
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- Zest of 1 lemon, plus the juice of half
- ½ tsp fine sea salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

**To finish**
- 100 g (⅓ cup) thick Greek yoghurt
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Run a knife horizontally through each breast and open it like a book so it lies at an even 2 cm.
2. In a bowl, mix the oil, za'atar, garlic, lemon zest and juice, salt and pepper into a loose paste. Toss the courgettes through half of it and spread them across the basket.
3. Rub the rest over the chicken, both sides, and lay the pieces on top of the courgettes without overlapping.
4. Air fry for 13 minutes. The chicken is done when it springs back under a finger and shows no pink at the thickest part — 74°C (165°F) on a probe. The courgette edges should be brown and shrunken.
5. Slice the chicken thickly across the grain, pile it onto the courgettes and finish with yoghurt, parsley and a squeeze of lemon.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Thighs need 15 minutes; thick aubergine slices stand in for courgette on the same timer.
- **Make it faster:** Ask for the breasts butterflied at the counter and you are down to 3 minutes of prep.
- **Bulk it out:** Couscous steeped in boiling water for 5 minutes while the basket runs, or warm pitta.
- **Leftovers:** Cold, chopped, with the courgettes and yoghurt as a lunchbox salad. Keeps 3 days chilled.
