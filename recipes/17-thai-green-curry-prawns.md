# 17 · Quick Thai Green Curry Prawns

**Cuisine:** Thai · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 15 min (5 prep / 10 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> The paste fries in the thick cream from the top of the tin until the oil splits out green — the difference between a proper curry and hot coconut soup.

## Why it works
Cracking the coconut cream and frying the paste in it toasts the lemongrass and galangal, giving depth you would otherwise only get from long simmering. Prawns go in for the final three minutes so they poach gently rather than tighten in a boiling pan.

## Ingredients
- 1 x 400 ml (14 fl oz) tin full-fat coconut milk, unshaken
- 3 tbsp Thai green curry paste
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) raw king prawns, peeled and deveined
- 150 g (5 oz) green beans, halved
- 1 courgette, in thick half-moons
- 100 g (3½ oz) sugar snap peas
- 1½ tbsp (22 ml) fish sauce
- 2 tsp palm sugar or soft brown sugar
- 3 makrut lime leaves, torn (optional)
- 1 lime, half juiced, half in wedges
- 20 g (1 cup, loosely packed) Thai basil leaves
- 1 red chilli, sliced

## Method
1. Open the tin without shaking and spoon the thick cream from the top into a cold, wide pan. Set the thin milk beneath aside.
2. Cook the cream over medium-high heat 2–3 minutes, stirring, until it thickens, sputters and green-tinged oil separates at the edges. Add the paste and fry 90 seconds, pressing it into the oil, until it smells fragrant and darkens.
3. Pour in the reserved milk with the fish sauce, sugar and lime leaves and bring to a gentle simmer.
4. Add the beans and courgette and simmer 4 minutes, then the sugar snaps and prawns for a final 3. The prawns are done as soon as they turn opaque and curl loosely; the beans should still snap.
5. Off the heat, stir in the lime juice and most of the basil, then taste and adjust with more fish sauce or lime. Scatter with chilli and the rest of the basil.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Chicken thigh strips need 8 minutes in the sauce; firm tofu and aubergine need 10.
- **Make it faster:** Frozen raw prawns go straight in — add them a minute earlier.
- **Bulk it out:** Jasmine rice, or rice noodles soaked while the curry simmers.
- **Leftovers:** Keeps 2 days. Reheat gently to steaming only, or the prawns turn rubbery.
