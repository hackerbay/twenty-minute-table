# 13 · Furikake Salmon Rice Bowl

**Cuisine:** Hawaiian-Japanese · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 15 min (5 prep / 10 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Salmon lacquered with soy and honey, flaked hot over warm rice, then buried under toasted sesame and torn nori.

## Why it works
Honey and soy brown far faster together than either does alone, so ten minutes in a hot basket builds a sticky glaze without drying the fillet past medium. Flaking the fish while it is hot lets the glaze run down into the rice instead of sitting on top.

## Ingredients

**For the basket**
- 4 salmon fillets (~150 g / 5 oz each), skin on
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) soy sauce
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) honey
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) rice vinegar
- 2 tsp finely grated fresh ginger
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

**For the bowls**
- 600 g (about 4 cups) cooked short-grain rice, warm
- 2 tbsp toasted white and black sesame seeds
- 2 sheets nori, torn into small pieces
- ½ tsp flaky sea salt
- 1 cucumber, halved and sliced on the diagonal
- 1 ripe avocado, sliced
- 3 spring onions, finely sliced

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Stir the soy, honey, vinegar, ginger and sesame oil together in a small bowl.
2. Sit the salmon skin-down in the basket and brush thickly with half the glaze. Air fry for 10 minutes, brushing on the rest at 6 minutes. It is done when the surface is dark and tacky and the thickest part flakes under light pressure; a little translucence at the centre is what you want.
3. Meanwhile, mix the sesame seeds, torn nori and flaky salt on your board. This is your furikake.
4. Divide the warm rice between four bowls. Flake the salmon off its skin over the top in large pieces, and spoon any glaze left in the basket over each.
5. Add the cucumber and avocado, then scatter generously with the furikake and spring onions.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Trout or thick mackerel fillets both take the glaze well; mackerel needs only 7 minutes.
- **Make it faster:** Cook a double batch of rice at the weekend — this bowl is built around rice you already have.
- **Bulk it out:** Add shelled edamame or quick-pickled radish, thinly sliced and left in rice vinegar while the salmon cooks.
- **Leftovers:** Keep salmon and rice separate and eat cold next day; reheating the rice under flaked fish makes it gluey.
