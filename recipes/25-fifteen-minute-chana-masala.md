# 25 · Fifteen-Minute Chana Masala

**Cuisine:** Indian · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 15 min (5 prep / 10 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Two tins of chickpeas and ten minutes at a proper heat, for a sauce that tastes cooked rather than merely warmed.

## Why it works
Frying the ground spices in hot oil for thirty seconds before the tomatoes arrive dissolves them into the fat, which is what separates a cooked-tasting curry from a dusty one. Crushing a quarter of the chickpeas against the side of the pan releases their starch and thickens the sauce without any simmering time.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) rapeseed oil or ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 20 g (¾ in) fresh ginger, grated
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp ground turmeric
- ½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
- 400 g (14 oz) tin chopped tomatoes
- 2 x 400 g (14 oz) tins chickpeas, drained
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp fine sea salt
- 1 lemon, halved
- 20 g (¾ cup, packed) coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a deep frying pan over a high heat. Add the cumin seeds, wait 20 seconds until they darken and smell nutty, then add the onion. Cook 3 minutes, stirring often, until browning at the edges.
2. Add the garlic, ginger and chilli for 1 minute, then the ground coriander, cumin, turmeric and chilli powder. Fry 30 seconds — no longer, or they will catch.
3. Tip in the tomatoes and cook 3 minutes over a high heat, stirring, until the sauce darkens and thickens enough to leave a trail behind the spoon.
4. Add the chickpeas, 150 ml (⅔ cup) water and the salt. Simmer 3 minutes, crushing about a quarter of the chickpeas against the pan side as you stir, until the sauce coats rather than pools.
5. Off the heat, stir in the garam masala and a good squeeze of lemon. Taste for salt and sharpness, then scatter with coriander.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Black-eyed beans or butter beans work the same way; butter beans crush more readily and thicken faster.
- **Make it faster:** Grate the onion instead of chopping and it softens in half the time.
- **Bulk it out:** A handful of spinach at step 4, with rice or chapatis alongside.
- **Leftovers:** Better on day two. Keeps 4 days chilled and freezes for 3 months.
