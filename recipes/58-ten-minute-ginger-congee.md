# 58 · Ten-Minute Ginger Congee

**Cuisine:** Chinese · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 15 min (4 prep / 11 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Cold rice from last night, pulsed until the grains fracture, then boiled hard until it turns silky.

## Why it works
Congee usually takes an hour because whole grains have to burst open from the inside. Pulsing cold cooked rice breaks them before they reach the pan, and a hard rolling boil rather than a gentle simmer keeps the released starch moving, so it thickens in ten minutes.

## Ingredients
- 500 g (3 cups) cold cooked rice, ideally short-grain or jasmine
- 1.2 litres (5 cups) water or unsalted chicken stock
- 30 g (1 oz) fresh ginger — half in coins, half finely shredded
- 1 tsp fine sea salt, plus more to taste
- 4 eggs
- 4 spring onions, sliced, whites and greens kept apart
- ½ tsp ground white pepper
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 40 g (¼ cup) roasted peanuts, crushed
- A small handful of coriander leaves

## Method
1. Put the cold rice in a blender jug with 300 ml (1¼ cups) of the water. Pulse four or five times, one second each: you want fractured grains and cloudy liquid, not a purée.
2. Tip it into a wide pan with the rest of the liquid, the ginger coins, the spring onion whites and the salt. Bring to a hard, rolling boil.
3. Boil 10 minutes, stirring every couple of minutes and scraping the base so nothing catches. It is ready when a spoon dragged across the bottom leaves a trail that closes slowly behind it.
4. Drop the heat, crack in the eggs and stir once so the whites ribbon through. Sixty seconds sets them.
5. Season with white pepper and more salt than you expect. Ladle into bowls and top with shredded ginger, spring onion greens, sesame oil, peanuts and coriander.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** No blender? Crush the cold rice against the side of the pan with a wooden spoon for a minute instead. Cooked pearl barley behaves the same way.
- **Make it faster:** Pulse the rice the night before and keep it in the fridge in its water.
- **Bulk it out:** Wilt in two handfuls of spinach at the end, or poach prawns for the last 3 minutes.
- **Leftovers:** Sets solid when chilled. Loosen with boiling water a ladleful at a time and boil for 2 minutes.
