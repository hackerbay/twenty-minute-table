# 11 · Berbere Sweet Potato & Lentil Bowl

**Cuisine:** Ethiopian · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 20 min (5 prep / 15 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Sweet potato gets a head start in the basket, then drained lentils drop in late to crisp against the hot metal.

## Why it works
Berbere is chilli-led but built on fenugreek and warm spices, which need fat and direct heat rather than a long simmer — in a hot oiled basket the blend blooms in minutes. Cutting the sweet potato to 2 cm brings it inside a fifteen-minute window, and holding the lentils back until the last five means they toast rather than collapse.

## Ingredients

**For the basket**
- 800 g (1¾ lb) sweet potatoes, scrubbed and cut into 2 cm (¾ in) cubes
- 2 x 400 g (14 oz) tins green or brown lentils, drained and patted dry
- 1½ tbsp berbere spice blend
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) olive oil
- 1 tsp fine sea salt
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced

**To finish**
- 200 g (¾ cup) thick Greek yoghurt
- 1 lemon — half juiced, half cut into wedges
- 200 g (7 oz) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 small red onion, very thinly sliced
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) coriander, roughly chopped

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Toss the sweet potato in the basket with 2 tbsp of the oil, 1 tbsp berbere and the salt until every cube is rust-coloured.
2. Air fry for 10 minutes, shaking hard at 5 minutes.
3. Add the dry lentils, garlic and remaining oil and berbere. Shake and air fry 5 minutes more, until a cube of sweet potato gives with no resistance and the lentils look dry and toasted rather than glossy.
4. Meanwhile stir the yoghurt with the lemon juice and a pinch of salt, and toss the tomatoes and onion together on your board.
5. Spoon the basket into four bowls, add the tomatoes and onion, spoon over the yoghurt and scatter with coriander. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Butternut squash or thick carrot batons run on the same timer. Chickpeas in place of lentils give a firmer, nuttier bite.
- **Make it faster:** Cut the sweet potato to 1.5 cm and the basket time drops to 11 minutes total.
- **Bulk it out:** Warm flatbread for scooping, or a pouch of brown rice stirred through the bowl.
- **Leftovers:** Keeps 3 days chilled and is better cold than reheated — dress with more lemon and eat as a salad.
