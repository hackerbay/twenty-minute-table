# 39 · Nasi Goreng Cauliflower Rice with Fried Egg

**Cuisine:** Indonesian · **Method:** Wok · **Total time:** 15 min (5 prep / 10 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Cauliflower rice, sambal and prawns fried hot and fast, with a crisp-edged egg on every plate.

## Why it works
Cauliflower rice releases water the moment it meets heat, so the wok must be hot enough to drive it off as steam — crowd it or drop the heat and you get porridge. Frying the shallots, garlic and sambal in oil first, before anything wet arrives, is what gives the dish depth.

## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) groundnut or sunflower oil
- 4 shallots, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp sambal oelek
- 250 g (9 oz) raw peeled prawns
- 800 g (1 lb 12 oz) cauliflower rice
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) soy sauce
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) honey
- 4 eggs
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- 2 tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp of the oil in a wok over a high heat. Fry the shallots and garlic 2 minutes, until deep gold at the edges, then stir in the sambal for 1 minute more — it should darken and smell toasted rather than sharp.
2. Add the prawns and toss 90 seconds, until just pink and curled into loose Cs. Tip the lot into a bowl.
3. Turn the heat as high as it goes and add the cauliflower rice in two batches, tossing constantly for 3 minutes in total. Listen to it: a steady sizzle means the water is going, silence means it is stewing.
4. Return the prawn mixture with the soy and honey and toss 1 minute, until every grain is coloured and the wok is dry again. Divide between plates.
5. Wipe the wok, add the last tablespoon of oil and fry the eggs over a high heat for 2 minutes, until the whites are lacy and brown at the rim, the yolks still running.
6. Sit an egg on each plate and add cucumber, tomato, spring onion and a lime wedge.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Diced chicken thigh instead of prawns needs 4 minutes. Leave both out for 200 g of firm tofu cubes.
- **Make it faster:** Frozen cauliflower rice goes in from the freezer and wants one extra minute.
- **Bulk it out:** Half cauliflower rice, half cold jasmine rice from the day before.
- **Leftovers:** Two days chilled, but fry the eggs fresh each time.
