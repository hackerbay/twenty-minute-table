# 27 · Green Shakshuka

**Cuisine:** Israeli / Levantine · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 15 min (5 prep / 10 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Eggs poached into a pan of collapsed greens, garlic and green chilli, feta melting into the hollows.

## Why it works
Spinach and chard carry enough water to steam themselves down in two minutes, giving you a wet, seasoned bed for the eggs without adding liquid you would then boil off. Pressing the wells down to the metal sets each white against the hot base while the yolk stays loose above it.

## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) olive oil
- 1 leek, halved lengthways and finely sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 2 green chillies, sliced (deseed for less heat)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 courgette, coarsely grated
- 400 g (14 oz) spinach or chard leaves, roughly chopped
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) coriander, stalks and leaves separated
- 8 eggs
- 150 g (5 oz) feta, crumbled
- 1 lemon, half juiced and half in wedges
- Salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a 28 cm (11 in) frying pan over medium-high. Add the leek and a pinch of salt and cook 2 minutes, until it slumps and turns bright green.
2. Add the garlic, chilli, cumin, ground coriander and chopped coriander stalks. Stir 1 minute, until the spices smell toasted.
3. Add the courgette and the greens in two handfuls, turning with tongs. Cook 2 minutes, until everything has collapsed and no water pools when you push the greens aside. Stir in the lemon juice and season.
4. Make eight wells with the back of a spoon, pressing down to the pan base. Crack an egg into each and scatter the feta into the gaps.
5. Cover with a lid or baking tray, drop the heat to low and cook 4–5 minutes, until the whites are opaque right up to the yolk but the yolks still shiver when you nudge the pan. Finish with pepper, coriander leaves and lemon wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Kale needs 2 extra minutes before the eggs go in. Ricotta salata stands in for feta.
- **Make it faster:** Bagged washed spinach and no courgette takes prep down to 3 minutes.
- **Bulk it out:** Warm flatbread in the dry pan before you start, or serve over crushed butter beans.
- **Leftovers:** Eggs do not reheat well. Make the greens base ahead — 3 days chilled, 2 minutes to reheat.
