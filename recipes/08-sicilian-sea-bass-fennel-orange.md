# 08 · Sicilian Sea Bass with Fennel, Olive & Orange

**Cuisine:** Italian (Sicilian) · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 14 min (4 prep / 10 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Fennel gets a four-minute head start, then the fish goes on top and the whole thing finishes together under a slick of orange and olive.

## Why it works
Fennel sliced thin enough to be translucent will soften in the same window as a fish fillet, but it still needs a head start to lose its raw crunch — four minutes alone does it. Sitting the bass skin-up on the fennel means the fillets steam gently from below while the skin dries and crisps above.

## Ingredients

**For the basket**
- 2 fennel bulbs (~500 g / 1 lb 2 oz), trimmed and sliced 5 mm thick
- 4 sea bass fillets (~130 g / 4½ oz each), skin on
- 100 g (¾ cup) green olives, squashed and stoned
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried chilli flakes
- Zest and juice of 1 orange
- ½ tsp fine sea salt

**To finish**
- 15 g (⅓ cup, packed) flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- Any reserved fennel fronds

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 190°C (375°F). Toss the fennel in the basket with 2 tbsp of the oil, the garlic, oregano, chilli, half the orange zest and the salt. Air fry for 4 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, pat the bass dry — skin will not crisp through moisture — and rub it with the remaining oil and a pinch of salt.
3. Shake the fennel, scatter over the olives, then lay the fillets on top, skin up, without overlapping.
4. Air fry for 6 minutes more. The fish is done when the flesh turns from glassy to white and the thickest part flakes cleanly under a fork; the skin should be tight and blistered.
5. Squeeze the orange juice over the fennel, not the skin, and finish with the rest of the zest, parsley and fronds.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Bream, red mullet or thin cod fillets all take the same 6 minutes. Capers instead of olives, a tablespoon, rinsed.
- **Make it faster:** Slice the fennel on the coarse side of a box grater or a mandoline in under a minute.
- **Bulk it out:** Boiled new potatoes crushed into the fennel juices, or bread for mopping.
- **Leftovers:** The fennel and olives are better the next day, cold, as a salad. Eat the fish the same evening.
