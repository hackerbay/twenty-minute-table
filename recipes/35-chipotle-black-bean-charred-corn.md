# 35 · Chipotle Black Bean & Charred Corn Skillet

**Cuisine:** Mexican · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 15 min (5 prep / 10 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Frozen corn hits a dry, smoking pan and blackens in three minutes flat. That char is the whole dish.

## Why it works
Corn browns before it properly thaws, so it goes into a bare pan first and takes its char while everything else waits on the board. The beans arrive already cooked, so the pan only has to warm them while the chipotle and their starchy liquor thicken to a sauce.

## Ingredients
- 400 g (14 oz) frozen sweetcorn
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil
- 1 red onion, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 chipotle chillies in adobo, chopped, plus 1 tbsp of their sauce
- 2 x 400 g (14 oz) tins black beans, drained but not rinsed
- 400 g (14 oz) tin chopped tomatoes
- ½ tsp fine sea salt
- 1 lime, halved
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) coriander, roughly chopped
- 100 g (3½ oz) feta, crumbled, or 1 avocado, sliced

## Method
1. Put a large frying pan over your highest heat with nothing in it. When it smokes faintly, tip in the frozen corn and spread it flat. Leave it 3 minutes, then stir once — you want blistered black patches, not even colour. Scrape into a bowl.
2. Add the oil, onion and a pinch of salt to the same pan. Fry 2 minutes, until the edges soften and colour.
3. Stir in the garlic, cumin, oregano and chipotle. Thirty seconds, until the spices smell toasted rather than raw.
4. Tip in the beans and tomatoes, season, and simmer hard for 4 minutes, crushing a few spoonfuls against the side of the pan. It is ready when a spoon dragged through leaves a clear channel for a second.
5. Fold the corn back through, squeeze over half the lime and taste for salt. Scatter with coriander and feta, and cut the rest into wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Pinto or kidney beans behave the same. No chipotle — 2 tsp smoked paprika and a pinch of cayenne gets you close.
- **Make it faster:** Char the corn while you dice the onion — it does not need watching.
- **Bulk it out:** Warm corn tortillas in the empty pan afterwards, or spoon it over baked sweet potatoes.
- **Leftovers:** Four days chilled, better on day two. Reheats with a splash of water.
