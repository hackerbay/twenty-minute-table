# 45 · Georgian Walnut-Garlic Green Beans with Eggs

**Cuisine:** Georgian · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 15 min (6 prep / 9 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Blanched beans in a raw walnut and garlic paste, with fried eggs on top so the yolks run into it.

## Why it works
Walnuts blitzed with garlic, vinegar and a splash of the bean cooking water make an instant emulsion — the nut oil and starchy water hold together the way a tahini sauce does, with no cooking at all. Blanching the beans for four minutes and running them under the cold tap stops them going khaki and keeps the squeak.

## Ingredients

**For the beans**
- 500 g (1 lb 2 oz) green beans, topped
- 8 eggs
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) olive oil

**For the walnut sauce**
- 100 g (1 cup) walnut halves
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) red wine vinegar
- ½ tsp ground coriander
- ¼ tsp ground fenugreek, blue fenugreek if you have it
- ¼ tsp cayenne or hot paprika, plus a pinch to finish
- 15 g (⅓ cup, packed) coriander leaves, plus more to serve
- ½ tsp fine sea salt

## Method
1. Bring a wide pan of well-salted water to the boil. Add the beans and cook 4 minutes — they should bend without snapping and still squeak against the teeth.
2. Meanwhile, put the walnuts, garlic, vinegar, spices, coriander and salt into a small blender or mortar and work to a coarse, sandy paste.
3. Scoop out 100 ml (scant ½ cup) of the bean water. Drain, run the beans under the cold tap for 20 seconds, then tip them into a serving bowl.
4. Loosen the paste with the reserved water a splash at a time until it falls from the spoon like thick double cream, then fold it through the warm beans.
5. Dry the pan, set it back over a medium-high heat with the oil and fry the eggs for 2 to 3 minutes, until the whites are set and lacy at the edges but the yolks still move.
6. Sit two eggs on each bowlful, finish with coriander and a pinch of cayenne.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Runner beans, broccoli or cooked beetroot take the same sauce — the pkhali principle.
- **Make it faster:** Pound the sauce while the water comes up; it is done before the beans are.
- **Bulk it out:** Warm flatbread, or a drained tin of butter beans folded in at step 4.
- **Leftovers:** The dressed beans keep 3 days chilled. Fry eggs fresh each time.
