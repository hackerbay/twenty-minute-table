# 86 · Charred Lemon Broccoli with Almonds

**Cuisine:** Italian · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 10 min (4 prep / 6 cook) · **Serves:** 4 · **Vegetarian**

> Broccoli with blackened tips, soft garlic chips and lemon slices that turn bitter-sweet and almost jammy in the heat.

## Why it works
Broccoli held close to a fierce dry heat loses its surface water fast, so the florets brown instead of going grey and squeaky. Slicing half the lemon into thin half-moons and cooking them alongside softens the peel and caramelises it, and you eat those slices whole — they season the dish far more sharply than juice on its own.

## Ingredients
- 500 g (1 lb 2 oz) broccoli, cut into small florets, stalk peeled and sliced
- 1 lemon — half cut into thin half-moons, half kept for squeezing
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 40 g (⅓ cup) flaked almonds
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- ½ tsp flaky sea salt

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Toss the broccoli, lemon slices, oil, chilli and salt in a bowl with your hands until every floret is glossy.
2. Tip into the basket in as close to a single layer as you can manage and air fry for 6 minutes, shaking hard at 3 minutes. Slot this in while your main rests, or run it straight after the basket has finished something else.
3. Add the garlic and almonds at the 4 minute mark, not before. Both go from pale to bitter in under a minute of extra heat.
4. It is done when the floret tips are properly black in places, the stalk gives to a knife with slight resistance, and the almonds are gold. Tip onto a platter and squeeze over the reserved lemon half.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Tenderstem broccoli, romanesco or thick spears of cauliflower on the same timer. Hazelnuts instead of almonds, roughly bashed.
- **Make it faster:** Leave out the almonds and slice the lemon while the fryer heats — three minutes of prep, and nothing to add mid-cook.
- **On the side:** Roast chicken thighs, a piece of grilled white fish, or sausages with white beans.
- **Leftovers:** Keeps 2 days chilled. Chop it small and fold it cold through pasta with the almonds and a little more oil.
