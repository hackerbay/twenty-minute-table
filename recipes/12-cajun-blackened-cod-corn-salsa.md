# 12 · Cajun Blackened Cod with Charred Corn Salsa

**Cuisine:** American (Louisiana) · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 12 min (4 prep / 8 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Cod under a thick coat of smoked paprika and cayenne, sweetcorn charring underneath, and a salsa stirred together in the residual heat.

## Why it works
Blackening is a dry rub meeting dry fish at high heat, so patting the fillets is the whole trick — surface water makes the spices steam into a paste instead of scorching into a crust. The corn sits on the basket floor, taking the most direct heat and blistering in exactly the eight minutes the cod needs.

## Ingredients

**For the basket**
- 4 thick cod loin fillets (~150 g / 5 oz each), patted very dry
- 300 g (2 cups) sweetcorn kernels, from 3 cobs or frozen and thawed
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp garlic granules
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper
- ½ tsp coarsely ground black pepper
- ¾ tsp fine sea salt
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil

**For the salsa**
- 1 small red onion, finely diced
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped
- 2 limes — 1 juiced, 1 cut into wedges
- 1 ripe avocado, diced
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Mix the paprika, oregano, thyme, garlic granules, cayenne, black pepper and salt on your board.
2. Rub the cod with 1 tbsp of the oil, then press the spice mix onto every surface until the fillets look genuinely dark, not dusted.
3. Toss the corn with the remaining oil and spread it flat in the basket. Sit the cod on top and air fry 8 minutes, until the rub is near-black at the edges and the fish parts into thick opaque petals under a fork.
4. Tip the hot corn into a bowl and stir through the onion, chilli, lime juice, avocado and coriander — the heat takes the raw edge off the onion.
5. Plate the cod, pile the salsa alongside and add lime wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Haddock, pollock or salmon take the same rub. Chicken thigh fillets need 14 minutes.
- **Make it faster:** Mix a jar of the rub in advance; it keeps six months and is just as good on prawns.
- **Bulk it out:** Warm corn tortillas, or rice put on as the basket goes in.
- **Leftovers:** Flake the cold fish through the remaining salsa with more lime for a next-day lunch.
