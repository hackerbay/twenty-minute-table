# 02 · Miso-Glazed Salmon with Charred Tenderstem

**Cuisine:** Japanese · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 12 min (3 prep / 9 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Three-ingredient glaze, one basket, nine minutes. The most reliable weeknight fish there is.

## Why it works
Miso is already fermented and salty, so it seasons and browns at the same time — no marinating window needed. The broccoli goes in underneath the fillets and catches the glaze as it drips.

## Ingredients
- 4 salmon fillets (~150 g / 5 oz each), skin on
- 300 g (10 oz) tenderstem broccoli or long-stem broccolini
- 2 tbsp white miso paste
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) mirin (or 2 tsp honey + 1 tsp water)
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) rice vinegar
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp finely grated fresh ginger
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 2 spring onions, finely sliced

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Stir the miso, mirin, vinegar and ginger together in a small bowl.
2. Toss the broccoli with the sesame oil and lay it flat in the basket. Sit the salmon fillets on top, skin down, and brush the tops thickly with the miso glaze.
3. Air fry for 9 minutes. The glaze should be dark and bubbling at the edges and the salmon should flake when pressed at its thickest point.
4. Lift onto plates, scatter with sesame seeds and spring onion, and spoon any glaze from the basket over the broccoli.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Cod, sea bass or thick-cut trout all work — drop to 7 minutes for thinner white fish. Firm tofu slabs need 12 minutes.
- **Make it faster:** Double the glaze and keep it in a jar; it lives happily in the fridge for a month.
- **Bulk it out:** A pouch of microwave brown rice or a handful of soba noodles cooked in 4 minutes.
- **Leftovers:** Excellent cold, flaked into a rice bowl with cucumber.
