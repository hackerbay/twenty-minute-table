# 37 · Moqueca Express — Brazilian Coconut Fish Stew

**Cuisine:** Brazilian · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 18 min (6 prep / 12 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Chunks of white fish dropped into a coconut and pepper broth and left completely alone until they turn opaque.

## Why it works
The fish goes in last and is never stirred, so it poaches in the coconut milk and holds together in proper chunks. Salting it with lime while you build the base draws out surface water and firms the flesh, which is the difference between chunks and flakes.

## Ingredients

**For the base**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil or red palm oil
- 1 onion, finely sliced
- 1 red and 1 yellow pepper, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 400 ml (1⅔ cups) tin full-fat coconut milk

**For the fish and finishing**
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) firm white fish (cod, hake or monkfish), cut into 4 cm (1½ in) chunks
- 1 lime — juice of half, the rest in wedges
- ¾ tsp fine sea salt
- 1 red chilli, sliced
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) coriander, stalks chopped, leaves left whole

## Method
1. Toss the fish with the lime juice and salt in a bowl and leave it while you cook the base.
2. Heat the oil in a wide, deep frying pan. Cook the onion and peppers over a medium-high heat for 5 minutes, until floppy and colouring at the edges.
3. Add the garlic, paprika and coriander stalks, stir 1 minute, then the tomatoes for 2 minutes more, pressing them down until they collapse.
4. Pour in the coconut milk, bring to a bare simmer and season. Bubble gently 2 minutes to thicken.
5. Slide the fish in in one layer, spoon sauce over the top, and cover. Cook 4 minutes without stirring. It is done when the chunks are opaque to the centre and part easily under a spoon.
6. Scatter with chilli and coriander leaves and serve from the pan with lime wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Raw prawns need 3 minutes, not 4. Firm tofu works, as does butternut given 8 minutes before the coconut milk goes in.
- **Make it faster:** Frozen sliced peppers go in straight from the bag.
- **Bulk it out:** Plain rice, or toasted cassava flour scattered over if you can find it.
- **Leftovers:** Two days chilled. Reheat gently — a hard boil breaks the fish up.
