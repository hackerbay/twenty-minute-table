# 10 · Shawarma-Spiced Turkey with Sumac Onions

**Cuisine:** Middle Eastern · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 15 min (5 prep / 10 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Turkey cut into thin strips crisps at the edges under a shawarma spicing, served with red onions gone soft and pink in nothing but sumac and lemon.

## Why it works
Cutting turkey breast into 1 cm strips triples the surface area, so the spices toast and the edges frizzle in ten minutes — the char a vertical spit gives, without the spit. Salting the onions with sumac and lemon while the basket runs draws out their bite, so they arrive sharp rather than raw and harsh.

## Ingredients

**For the basket**
- 700 g (1½ lb) turkey breast steaks, cut into 1 cm (½ in) strips
- 2½ tbsp (38 ml) olive oil
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1½ tsp sweet paprika
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp ground turmeric
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 tsp fine sea salt

**For the sumac onions**
- 2 red onions, halved and sliced as thinly as you can
- 2 tsp sumac
- Juice of ½ lemon
- ¼ tsp fine sea salt
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) flat-leaf parsley, chopped

**To finish**
- 200 g (¾ cup) thick Greek yoghurt
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Toss the turkey strips in a bowl with the oil, all the spices, garlic, lemon juice and salt until evenly ochre.
2. Scatter the strips loosely across the basket in a rough single layer — a heap will steam and stay pale.
3. Air fry for 10 minutes, shaking hard at 5 minutes to separate any strips that have knitted together.
4. Meanwhile, put the onions on the board, sprinkle with sumac and salt, squeeze over the lemon and scrunch until they slacken and turn magenta. Fold through the parsley.
5. The turkey is done when the thinnest strips are browned and the thickest opaque all through. Pile onto plates with the onions, spoon over yoghurt and add lemon wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Chicken thigh strips take 12 minutes; sliced halloumi or cauliflower steaks take 12 as well.
- **Make it faster:** Buy turkey mini fillets and slice them lengthways — two cuts each and the prep is done.
- **Bulk it out:** Flatbreads, or bulgur soaked in boiling water for 10 minutes while the basket runs.
- **Leftovers:** Excellent cold in a wrap with the onions. Keeps 3 days chilled.
