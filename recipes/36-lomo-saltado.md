# 36 · Lomo Saltado

**Cuisine:** Peruvian · **Method:** Wok · **Total time:** 15 min (6 prep / 9 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Beef, onion and tomato seared hard in a wok, splashed with vinegar and soy, with the chips folded straight back in.

## Why it works
The wok has to be properly hot and the beef dry and in one layer, or it steams grey instead of browning. Microwaving the potatoes while you slice everything means they need two minutes in hot oil to crisp rather than a full deep-fry.

## Ingredients
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) waxy potatoes, cut into 1 cm (½ in) chips
- 500 g (1 lb 2 oz) beef sirloin or rump, cut into finger-thick strips
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) groundnut or sunflower oil
- 1 large red onion, cut into thick wedges
- 3 tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 1 red chilli, sliced, or 1 tbsp ají amarillo paste
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) soy sauce
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) red wine vinegar
- ½ tsp coarsely ground black pepper
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) coriander, roughly chopped

## Method
1. Put the potatoes in a bowl with a splash of water, cover, and microwave on full for 6 minutes while you slice everything else. Drain and pat dry — dry chips crisp, wet ones spit.
2. Heat 2 tbsp of the oil in a wok until it shimmers. Fry the potatoes 3 minutes, turning twice, until golden and crisp on two sides. Tip them back into the bowl.
3. Add the last tablespoon of oil and the beef, spread flat. Leave 60 seconds undisturbed for a crust, then toss a minute more. Push it up the side of the wok.
4. Throw the onion, chilli and garlic into the base. Two minutes on high — smoky at the edges, still snapping in the middle.
5. Add the tomatoes, soy, vinegar and pepper. Toss for 1 minute, until the tomatoes slump and the liquid turns glossy and clings to the beef rather than pooling.
6. Fold the potatoes back in with the coriander and serve straight from the wok.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Skirt or bavette, sliced across the grain. Thick portobello strips make a good meat-free version.
- **Make it faster:** Freeze the beef 15 minutes first and it slices cleanly in half the time.
- **Bulk it out:** White rice alongside is traditional and soaks up what the potatoes miss.
- **Leftovers:** Two days chilled. The chips soften, so reheat hard in the wok, never the microwave.
