# 15 · Salt & Pepper Tofu with Pak Choi

**Cuisine:** Chinese (Cantonese) · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 18 min (6 prep / 12 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Cornflour-dusted tofu goes craggy in the basket, pak choi joins late, and the garlic and chilli go in at the very end so they toast rather than scorch.

## Why it works
A thin coat of cornflour pulls surface moisture off the tofu and sets into a shell, giving the shattery edges a deep-fryer would — without the litre of oil. The aromatics go in for the last two minutes only, because sliced garlic and chilli turn bitter long before tofu is ready.

## Ingredients

**For the basket**
- 2 x 400 g (14 oz) blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed briefly and cut into 3 cm (1¼ in) cubes
- 3 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch)
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) groundnut or vegetable oil
- 300 g (10 oz) pak choi, halved lengthways
- 5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 2 red chillies, sliced
- 4 spring onions, cut into 3 cm lengths

**For the salt and pepper mix**
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns, toasted and crushed
- 1 tsp black peppercorns, crushed
- ½ tsp ground white pepper
- 1 tsp flaky sea salt
- ½ tsp caster sugar

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Roll the tofu in the cornflour on your board until evenly dusty, then tip into the basket and toss with 2 tbsp of the oil.
2. Air fry 8 minutes, shaking firmly at 4. The cubes should sound light and rattle against the basket.
3. Meanwhile, crush the peppercorns and mix with the white pepper, salt and sugar.
4. Toss the pak choi with the remaining oil, add to the basket and air fry 2 minutes. Add the garlic, chilli and spring onions and air fry 2 minutes more — the tofu deep gold, the pak choi stems still squeaky at the base.
5. Tip everything into a warm bowl, scatter the salt and pepper mix over while hot and toss once so it clings. Serve immediately.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** King oyster mushrooms in thick coins take the same treatment and timings. Prawns need only 5 minutes.
- **Make it faster:** Skip the pressing — extra-firm tofu blotted hard with a tea towel is dry enough.
- **Bulk it out:** Steamed jasmine rice, or plain noodles tossed with the seasoned oil from the basket.
- **Leftovers:** The coating softens overnight. Bring it back with 4 minutes at 200°C rather than a microwave.
