# 69 · Danish Rye with Cottage Cheese, Cucumber & Dill

**Cuisine:** Danish · **Method:** No cook · **Total time:** 8 min (8 prep / 0 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Dense dark rye, cold cottage cheese and enough dill to smell green from across the kitchen.

## Why it works
Salting the cucumber ribbons and leaving them while you build the rest draws out the water that would otherwise run straight into the bread. Rye this dense needs no toasting; the oil and the fat in the cottage cheese are what carry it, and a hard grind of black pepper does the rest.

## Ingredients

**For the base**
- 8 slices dense dark rye bread (rugbrød), about 90 g (3 oz) per person
- 400 g (1¾ cups) cottage cheese
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) extra-virgin olive oil

**For the top**
- 1 cucumber
- 8 radishes, sliced into thin coins
- 20 g (¾ cup, loosely packed) dill, fronds picked
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 20 g (2 tbsp) pumpkin seeds
- ½ tsp flaky sea salt
- Coarsely ground black pepper

## Method
1. Run a peeler down the length of the cucumber to make long ribbons, stopping when you reach the seedy core. Pile them on the board, toss with the flaky salt and leave for 5 minutes.
2. Lay the rye out, two slices per plate. Spoon the cottage cheese on in rough heaps rather than spreading it flat, so there are ridges for the oil to sit in.
3. Squeeze the cucumber ribbons gently over the sink. A surprising amount of water comes out and they should feel limp and glassy. Toss them with the lemon juice.
4. Drape the ribbons over the cottage cheese, tuck the radish coins in among them, and scatter with dill, pumpkin seeds and lemon zest.
5. Trickle over the olive oil, add a hard grind of black pepper, and eat with a knife and fork while the cucumber is still cold.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Thick natural yoghurt or ricotta in place of the cottage cheese. Sourdough stands in for the rye, though you lose the sour depth.
- **Make it faster:** Cut the cucumber into coins rather than ribbons and skip the salting entirely.
- **Bulk it out:** Half a sliced avocado per plate, or a third slice of rye for anyone walking to work.
- **Leftovers:** Assembled, it softens within the hour. The dressed cucumber and dill keep two days in a covered bowl.
