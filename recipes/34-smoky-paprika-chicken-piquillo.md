# 34 · Smoky Paprika Chicken with Piquillo Peppers

**Cuisine:** Spanish · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 18 min (6 prep / 12 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Chicken thighs rubbed with two kinds of pimentón, browned hard, then loosened with sherry vinegar and torn piquillo peppers.

## Why it works
Thigh meat browns without drying out, so you can leave it alone on high heat for four minutes and build real colour on the pan base. Sherry vinegar then lifts all of that into the sauce in seconds, doing the work a long braise would otherwise be needed for.

## Ingredients
- 700 g (1 lb 9 oz) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3 cm pieces
- 1 tsp sweet smoked paprika
- ½ tsp hot smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 bay leaf
- 220 g (8 oz) drained piquillo peppers, torn into strips
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) sherry vinegar
- 100 ml (scant ½ cup) good chicken stock
- 30 g (¼ cup) almonds, toasted and roughly chopped
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Toss the chicken on the board with both paprikas, a good pinch of salt and plenty of pepper until every piece is coated rust red.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over high. Add the chicken in one layer and leave it alone for 4 minutes — moving it early costs you the crust. Turn and cook 2 minutes more.
3. Drop the heat to medium and add the onion, garlic and bay. Cook 3 minutes, until the onion softens at the edges but keeps some bite.
4. Add the peppers and vinegar, scraping the base clean as it bubbles. Pour in the stock and simmer 3 minutes, until it reduces to a glossy coating and the thickest piece of chicken shows no pink at the centre.
5. Off the heat, scatter with almonds and parsley. Taste, and add another splash of vinegar if it needs sharpening.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Roasted red peppers from a jar if you cannot find piquillos. Chickpeas make it meat-free.
- **Make it faster:** Ask the butcher to dice the thighs, or buy them ready-cut.
- **Bulk it out:** Serve with bread, or stir in a drained tin of white beans with the stock.
- **Leftovers:** Keeps 3 days chilled and reheats well. The paprika deepens overnight.
