# 28 · Greek Lemon Chicken & Orzo

**Cuisine:** Greek · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 20 min (5 prep / 15 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> The orzo cooks in the pan with the chicken, drinking up the browned bits and the stock, and comes out creamy without a drop of cream.

## Why it works
Orzo cooks through in nine minutes in a shallow layer of stock, and the starch it sheds thickens that stock into a sauce — the risotto trick without the stirring. Searing the chicken first leaves a brown film on the pan base that the stock lifts into every grain.

## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) olive oil
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 2 cm strips
- 6 spring onions, sliced (or 1 onion, finely chopped)
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 300 g (1½ cups) orzo
- 750 ml (3 cups) good chicken stock, hot
- 2 lemons — zest of both, juice of one, the rest in wedges
- 150 g (5 oz) spinach
- 100 g (3½ oz) feta, crumbled
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) dill, chopped
- Salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a wide, deep sauté pan over high heat. Season the chicken and lay it in one layer. Sear 4 minutes without moving it, until the undersides are properly browned — it will not be cooked through yet.
2. Add the spring onions, garlic and oregano and stir 1 minute, until fragrant.
3. Tip in the orzo and stir 30 seconds to coat every grain in oil. Pour in the hot stock, bring to a boil and scrape the pan base clean with a wooden spoon.
4. Simmer briskly 9 minutes, stirring every minute and reaching into the corners so nothing catches. It is done when the orzo is tender with a slight bite at the centre and the liquid holds a line behind the spoon.
5. Off the heat, fold through the lemon zest and juice, then the spinach — it wilts in the residual heat in under a minute. Scatter with feta and dill, add pepper and serve with lemon wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Prawns instead of chicken — stir them in for the orzo's last 3 minutes.
- **Make it faster:** Boil the stock in the kettle while you cut the chicken.
- **Bulk it out:** A drained tin of butter beans at step 4, plus 100 ml more stock.
- **Leftovers:** Keeps 2 days chilled. Reheat with a splash of water — orzo drinks as it sits.
