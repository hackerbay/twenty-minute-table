# 26 · Turkish Menemen

**Cuisine:** Turkish · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 12 min (4 prep / 8 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Soft green peppers cooked down in olive oil with grated tomato, then eggs stirred through until barely set.

## Why it works
The peppers go in first and get four full minutes alone in the oil, which is what turns them sweet instead of sharp and green. The eggs are broken in with a fork and moved slowly, so they set in soft ribbons through the sauce rather than scrambling into dry lumps.

## Ingredients
- 4 tbsp (60 ml) olive oil
- 3 long green peppers (or 2 green bell peppers), deseeded and sliced
- 6 ripe tomatoes, coarsely grated and skins discarded (or a 400 g / 14 oz tin of chopped tomatoes)
- 1 tsp pul biber (Aleppo pepper), plus more to finish
- ½ tsp fine sea salt
- 8 large eggs
- 20 g (¾ cup, packed) flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- crusty bread, to serve

## Method
1. Heat the olive oil in a wide frying pan over a medium heat. Add the peppers and cook 4 minutes, stirring now and then, until they are limp and blistered at the edges.
2. Add the tomatoes, pul biber and salt. Turn the heat up and cook 3 minutes, until the liquid has gone and the oil comes back to the surface in shiny pools.
3. Crack the eggs straight into the pan. Break the yolks with a fork and stir once or twice, leaving the whites streaky rather than beaten in.
4. Turn the heat low and move the eggs slowly with the fork for 90 seconds, pulling from the outside in. Stop while they still look glossy and a little underdone — the hot pan finishes them.
5. Scatter with parsley and more pul biber, and take the pan straight to the table with the bread.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Crumbled white cheese stirred in at step 3, or a spoon of thick yoghurt on the side.
- **Make it faster:** Grate whole tomatoes on the coarse side of a box grater — it skips peeling and the chopping board both.
- **Bulk it out:** More eggs; the pan takes ten. A second pepper stretches it further.
- **Leftovers:** Eat it now. Menemen neither keeps nor reheats well.
