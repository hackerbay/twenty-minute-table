# 42 · Ethiopian Gomen with Spiced Eggs

**Cuisine:** Ethiopian · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 15 min (4 prep / 11 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Greens braised soft with ginger and berbere, with eggs cracked into wells in the pan and lidded until just set.

## Why it works
Gomen is traditionally collards cooked for an hour, but shredding kale finely across the leaf gets the same silky texture in eight minutes, because all those cut edges take up water fast. Berbere needs a moment in hot fat before any liquid arrives, or it stays gritty and tastes only of heat.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil or ghee
- 1 onion, finely sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 4 cm (1½ in) ginger, finely chopped
- 2 tsp berbere, plus a pinch to finish
- 500 g (1 lb 2 oz) kale or collard greens, stalks stripped, leaves finely shredded
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 150 ml (⅔ cup) water
- ½ tsp fine sea salt
- 8 eggs
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium-high heat and fry the onion 3 minutes, until translucent and catching at the edges.
2. Add the garlic, ginger and berbere and stir 45 seconds. The fat should turn brick red and the spice smell sweet and toasted rather than sharp.
3. Pile in the kale with the tomatoes, water and salt. It will look like far too much; press it under a lid for 30 seconds and it collapses to a third.
4. Cook uncovered 5 minutes, stirring twice, until the greens are dark and glossy, tender enough to cut with the side of a spoon, and the water has gone.
5. Make eight shallow wells with the back of the spoon and crack an egg into each. Season, cover, and cook 3 minutes for runny yolks, 4 for set.
6. Finish with a pinch more berbere and serve from the pan with lemon wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Cavolo nero, spring greens or chard all work — chard needs only 3 minutes. No berbere: 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp chilli, ½ tsp fenugreek, a pinch of cinnamon.
- **Make it faster:** Bagged shredded kale skips the stripping and shredding entirely.
- **Bulk it out:** Injera if you have it, flatbread if not, or a drained tin of chickpeas added at step 3.
- **Leftovers:** The greens keep 3 days chilled and reheat well. Cook the eggs to order.
