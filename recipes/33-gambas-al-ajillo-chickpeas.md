# 33 · Gambas al Ajillo with Chickpeas

**Cuisine:** Spanish · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 12 min (4 prep / 8 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Sliced garlic brought up slowly in a lot of olive oil, then chickpeas blistered in it and prawns cooked for barely two minutes.

## Why it works
Garlic goes into cold oil and heats with the pan, so it perfumes the whole lot on the way up instead of burning in the first thirty seconds. Drying the chickpeas before they hit the oil is what lets them blister and take on colour rather than steam in their own moisture.

## Ingredients
- 75 ml (5 tbsp) olive oil
- 8 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 2 dried guindilla chillies, snapped in half (or 1 tsp chilli flakes)
- 2 x 400 g (14 oz) tins chickpeas, drained and patted dry
- 1 tsp sweet smoked paprika
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) raw king prawns, peeled
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) dry sherry or dry white wine
- 1 lemon
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- Flaky sea salt

## Method
1. Put the oil, garlic and chilli into a cold, wide frying pan and set it over medium heat. Let it come up slowly for 3 minutes, until the garlic is the colour of straw and the oil smells sweet. Starting cold is what stops it catching.
2. Add the chickpeas, paprika and a pinch of salt. Fry 3 minutes, pressing some against the pan base with the back of a spoon, until their skins blister and split.
3. Lay the prawns in one layer and salt them. Cook 1 minute, turn, then 1 minute more — they are done the moment they turn pink and curl loosely. Any longer and they tighten.
4. Pour in the sherry. Let it hiss and reduce for 30 seconds, scraping the base so nothing is left behind.
5. Off the heat, add a good squeeze of lemon, the parsley and flaky salt. Bring the pan to the table while the oil is still bubbling at the edges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Squid rings instead of prawns — 90 seconds, no more. Butter beans in place of chickpeas.
- **Make it faster:** Peeled prawns and drained chickpeas straight from the fridge takes prep to 2 minutes.
- **Bulk it out:** Bread is the point here — plenty of it, for the garlic oil left in the pan.
- **Leftovers:** The chickpeas keep 3 days and are good cold. Eat the prawns the same day.
