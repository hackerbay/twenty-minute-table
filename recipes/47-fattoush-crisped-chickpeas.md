# 47 · Fattoush with Crisped Chickpeas

**Cuisine:** Lebanese · **Method:** No cook · **Total time:** 15 min (15 prep / 0 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> The crunch normally comes from fried bread; here it comes from chickpeas rubbed out of their skins and air-dried while you chop.

## Why it works
Chickpea skins trap water and go slippery in dressing, so rubbing the drained tins in a tea towel, tipping the skins away and leaving the bare chickpeas to dry with salt and sumac for ten minutes gives a firm, nubbly bite with no pan involved. Salting the tomatoes meanwhile does the opposite job on purpose — the juice they give up becomes half the dressing.

## Ingredients

**For the salad**
- 2 x 400 g (14 oz) tins chickpeas, drained
- 2 tsp sumac, plus a pinch to finish
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) extra virgin olive oil
- 400 g (14 oz) ripe tomatoes, cut into rough chunks
- 1 cucumber, halved and thickly sliced
- 6 radishes, quartered
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 2 baby gem lettuces, torn
- 30 g (¾ cup, packed) flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 15 g (⅓ cup, packed) mint leaves
- 40 g (⅓ cup) toasted pine nuts

**For the dressing**
- juice of 1½ lemons
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1 tbsp pomegranate molasses
- 1 tsp sumac
- ½ tsp fine sea salt

## Method
1. Tip the chickpeas onto a clean tea towel, fold it over and rub firmly for 20 seconds. Open it out, pick away the loose skins.
2. Spread them on a plate, scatter over 2 tsp sumac, a pinch of salt and 1 tbsp oil, and leave uncovered while you do everything else.
3. Put the tomatoes in the serving bowl with a good pinch of salt; within two minutes they weep.
4. Whisk the dressing and the remaining 2 tbsp oil straight into the tomatoes and their juice.
5. Add the cucumber, radishes, spring onions, lettuce and herbs and turn it by hand twice, no more — fattoush bruises if you work it.
6. Fold through most of the chickpeas, scatter the rest over with the pine nuts and more sumac.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Butter beans instead of chickpeas — no skinning, just thorough drying.
- **Make it faster:** Skip the skinning and pat the chickpeas bone dry. Less crunch, four minutes saved.
- **Bulk it out:** 200 g crumbled feta, or a tin of tuna in olive oil.
- **Leftovers:** Best within the hour. To keep it, hold back the lettuce and herbs, add them fresh.
