# 16 · Pad Krapow — Thai Basil Chicken

**Cuisine:** Thai · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 12 min (5 prep / 7 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Chicken chopped by hand rather than minced, hit hard with garlic and bird's eye chilli, finished with two handfuls of basil off the heat.

## Why it works
Chopping thigh meat by knife leaves ragged pieces that brown in patches and stay juicy, where mince packs together and steams. The basil goes in off the heat — holy basil loses its peppery, clove-like edge within about thirty seconds of direct cooking.

## Ingredients
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) boneless chicken thighs, skin removed
- 6 garlic cloves
- 3–4 bird's eye chillies, more if you like heat
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) groundnut or vegetable oil
- 1½ tbsp (22 ml) fish sauce
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) light soy sauce
- 2 tsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tsp palm sugar or soft brown sugar
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) water
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 100 g (3½ oz) green beans, cut into 2 cm lengths
- 40 g (2 cups, loosely packed) holy basil or Thai basil leaves

## Method
1. Chop the chicken into rough 1 cm pieces — coarse and uneven is right. Flatten the garlic and chillies with the side of the knife and chop to a rough paste.
2. Stir the fish sauce, both soys, sugar and water in a small bowl.
3. Get a large frying pan very hot, add the oil, then the garlic and chilli. Stir 15 seconds, until it smells sharp and the garlic is just turning pale gold.
4. Add the chicken and spread it out. Leave 90 seconds without stirring to take colour, then stir-fry 3 minutes until no pink remains.
5. Add the onion, beans and sauce. Cook hard for 2 minutes, until the liquid reduces to a glossy coating and the beans are still bright.
6. Off the heat, throw in the basil and stir just until it wilts. Serve straight away over rice.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Pork shoulder, turkey thigh or crumbled firm tofu all work. Swap the dark soy for 1 tbsp oyster sauce for a rounder version.
- **Make it faster:** Chop the garlic and chilli paste in bulk and freeze in teaspoon blobs.
- **Bulk it out:** Jasmine rice and a fried egg with lacy edges, cooked in the same pan after.
- **Leftovers:** Keeps 3 days and reheats well, though the basil fades — add fresh leaves at the table.
