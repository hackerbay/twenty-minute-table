# 03 · Peri-Peri Chicken Thighs with Blistered Peppers

**Cuisine:** Portuguese-Mozambican · **Method:** Air fryer · **Total time:** 18 min (5 prep / 13 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> A peri-peri rub built from two chillies and a lemon, blistering onto chicken while the peppers soften in the drippings underneath.

## Why it works
Boneless thighs opened out flat cook through in thirteen minutes and stay juicy where breast would tighten and dry. The vinegar and lemon keep the rub loose enough to coat every surface, so it browns into a lacquer rather than pooling in the drawer.

## Ingredients

**For the basket**
- 800 g (1¾ lb) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 3 romano peppers (~450 g / 1 lb), deseeded and cut into thick strips
- 1 red onion, cut into eight wedges

**For the peri-peri rub**
- 2 red bird's eye chillies, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) red wine vinegar
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp fine sea salt

**To finish**
- 15 g (⅓ cup, packed) flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Heat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Open the thighs out on the board and press the thick lobes flat so each is an even 2 cm (¾ in).
2. Stir the chilli, garlic, paprika, oregano, vinegar, oil, lemon zest and juice and salt in a bowl. Add the chicken and turn it until every piece is streaked red.
3. Spread the peppers and onion across the basket, lay the chicken on top in a single layer and scrape the last of the rub over.
4. Air fry for 13 minutes, turning the chicken once at 8 minutes. It is ready when the edges have caught dark and the juices run clear at the thickest point — 74°C (165°F) on a probe.
5. Let it settle for the minute it takes to find plates, then serve with the peppers, the pan juices, parsley and lemon wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Drumsticks work on the same rub but need 20 minutes. Halloumi or thick portobello slices take 10.
- **Make it faster:** Double the rub and keep half in a jar; it holds two weeks chilled and cuts prep to two minutes.
- **Bulk it out:** Warm flatbreads in the hot empty basket for 60 seconds, or serve over rice.
- **Leftovers:** Shred the cold chicken through the peppers with more lemon. Keeps 3 days chilled.
