# 21 · Vietnamese Lemongrass Chicken Bowl

**Cuisine:** Vietnamese · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 15 min (8 prep / 7 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Chicken thighs cooked hard in lemongrass and fish sauce, tipped over cold noodles, cucumber and a heap of soft herbs.

## Why it works
Fish sauce and honey reduce in the pan to a lacquer on the meat, so the bowl needs nothing beyond a sharp lime dressing. Thighs carry enough fat to take a fierce heat for seven minutes without drying, which is what gives you the dark caught edges.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken**
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) boneless chicken thighs, cut into 3 cm pieces
- 2 lemongrass stalks, outer layers stripped, cores very finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) fish sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- ½ tsp coarse black pepper
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) rapeseed oil

**For the bowls**
- 200 g (7 oz) dried rice vermicelli
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) lime juice
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 red chilli, finely sliced
- 2 carrots, coarsely grated
- 1 cucumber, cut into batons
- 30 g (1 cup, packed) mint and coriander leaves
- 40 g (¼ cup) roasted peanuts, crushed

## Method
1. Boil the kettle. Toss the chicken with the lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, honey and pepper in a bowl.
2. Put the vermicelli in a large heatproof bowl, cover with boiling water and leave 4 minutes, until pliable with a little bite. Drain, rinse cold and leave in the colander.
3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high. Spread the chicken out and leave it 3 minutes without stirring, until the undersides are dark. Turn and cook 4 minutes more, until firm and the juices run clear.
4. Whisk the lime juice, fish sauce, honey, chilli and 3 tbsp water in the emptied noodle bowl.
5. Divide the noodles between bowls with the carrot, cucumber and herbs. Add the chicken, spoon over the dressing and scatter with peanuts.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Pork shoulder steaks, sliced thin, cook in the same time. Firm tofu needs 6 minutes in a drier pan.
- **Make it faster:** Blitz the lemongrass, garlic and chilli together while the kettle boils.
- **Bulk it out:** Double the noodles, or add shredded little gem and raw beansprouts.
- **Leftovers:** Keep the dressing separate and it holds 2 days. Good cold, straight from the fridge.
