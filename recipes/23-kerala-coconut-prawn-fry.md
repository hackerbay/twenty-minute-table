# 23 · Kerala Coconut Prawn Fry

**Cuisine:** Indian (South) · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 15 min (7 prep / 8 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Mustard seeds popping in coconut oil, curry leaves crackling, prawns in and out of the pan in three minutes.

## Why it works
Prawns need barely three minutes, so the masala is built first and they go in last, onto a base that is already sweet and softened. The grated coconut goes in after that, where residual heat toasts it just enough to cling to the prawns.

## Ingredients
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) raw king prawns, peeled and patted dry
- ½ tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
- 1 tsp coarsely cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp fine sea salt
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) coconut oil
- 1 tsp black mustard seeds
- 20 fresh curry leaves
- 4 shallots, finely sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 20 g (¾ in) fresh ginger, finely chopped
- 2 green chillies, split lengthways
- 60 g (¾ cup) fresh or frozen grated coconut
- 1 lime, halved
- 15 g (½ cup, packed) coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Toss the prawns with the turmeric, chilli powder, pepper and salt in a bowl.
2. Heat the coconut oil in a wide frying pan over a medium-high heat. Add the mustard seeds and wait for them to pop, about 30 seconds, then the curry leaves — they will spit and crackle.
3. Add the shallots and cook 4 minutes, stirring, until soft and edged with brown. Add the garlic, ginger and chillies and stir 1 minute, until the raw smell goes.
4. Turn the heat high and spread the prawns out in one layer. Leave them 90 seconds, then toss and cook 60–90 seconds more, until they curl into loose C shapes and are opaque right through. A tight O means they have gone too far.
5. Scatter in the coconut and toss a minute, until it smells toasted and clings. Off the heat, squeeze over the lime and stir through the coriander.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Firm white fish in 3 cm chunks needs 4 minutes and gentler turning. Squid rings take 90 seconds flat.
- **Make it faster:** Frozen raw prawns thaw in 5 minutes in cold water while you slice the shallots.
- **Bulk it out:** Rice, or 200 g spinach wilted into the pan just before the prawns go in.
- **Leftovers:** 2 days chilled. Warm gently — a hard reheat turns prawns rubbery.
