# 31 · Tuscan White Bean, Kale & Lemon Skillet

**Cuisine:** Italian (Tuscan) · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 12 min (4 prep / 8 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Cannellini beans crushed into their own broth with rosemary, garlic and cavolo nero, sharpened hard with lemon at the end.

## Why it works
Crushing a quarter of the beans against the side of the pan releases their starch, which turns a thin stock into something that coats — no cream, no flour, no extra pan. Adding the lemon off the heat keeps its edge bright, where simmering would flatten it into something dull.

## Ingredients
- 4 tbsp (60 ml) olive oil, plus more to finish
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp rosemary leaves, finely chopped
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 4 anchovy fillets in olive oil (optional)
- 200 g (7 oz) cavolo nero or kale, stalks stripped, leaves shredded
- 2 x 400 g (14 oz) tins cannellini beans, drained
- 150 ml (⅔ cup) good chicken or vegetable stock
- 1 lemon — zest and juice
- 40 g (1½ oz) Parmesan or pecorino, finely grated
- Salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium. Add the garlic, rosemary, chilli and anchovies if using. Cook 2 minutes, pressing the anchovies until they dissolve. The garlic should be pale gold, never brown.
2. Add the kale and a pinch of salt and toss 2 minutes, until the leaves darken to deep green and slump into the oil.
3. Tip in the beans and stock. Simmer 3 minutes, crushing about a quarter of the beans against the side of the pan with a wooden spoon, until the liquid thickens and clings to the leaves rather than running off them.
4. Off the heat, stir in the lemon zest and juice and most of the cheese. Taste and season — tinned beans need more salt than you expect.
5. Serve straight from the pan with the rest of the cheese, a thread of oil and plenty of black pepper.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Butter beans or borlotti both work. Spinach instead of kale needs only 30 seconds at step 2.
- **Make it faster:** Use bagged shredded kale and you are down to 2 minutes of prep.
- **Bulk it out:** Spoon over toasted sourdough rubbed with a raw garlic clove, or top with a fried egg.
- **Leftovers:** Better on day two, once the rosemary has settled in. Keeps 4 days chilled; loosen with water.
