# 41 · Burmese Golden Turmeric Chicken

**Cuisine:** Burmese · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 15 min (5 prep / 10 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Onions cooked down in turmeric oil until the fat itself turns orange, with thin-sliced chicken finished in the same slick.

## Why it works
Burmese cooking builds its base by frying onion, garlic and turmeric in more oil than feels sensible, until the oil takes on colour and separates — that stained oil is the sauce. Slicing the thighs thin means the chicken cooks through in the four minutes the onions need to soften.

## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) groundnut oil
- 2 onions, finely sliced
- 6 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 4 cm (1½ in) ginger, finely chopped
- 2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp paprika
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, sliced 1 cm (½ in) thick
- 3 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) fish sauce
- 100 ml (scant ½ cup) good chicken stock
- 1 red chilli, sliced
- 20 g (½ cup, packed) coriander, roughly chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium-high heat. Add the onions with a pinch of salt and fry 4 minutes, stirring, until soft and browning at the tips.
2. Add the garlic, ginger, turmeric and paprika and stir 1 minute. The oil should turn deep orange and the dusty, raw smell of the turmeric disappear.
3. Turn the heat up, add the chicken and toss until every piece is coated in the golden oil. Spread it flat and leave it 2 minutes.
4. Add the tomatoes, fish sauce and stock. Simmer 3 minutes, pressing the tomatoes down as they soften, until the sauce is thick enough to sit on the chicken and the oil pools orange at the sides.
5. Check the thickest slice — white through, no pink at the centre. Scatter with chilli and coriander and serve with lime wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Chicken breast works, sliced thinner, with the simmer dropped to 2 minutes. White fish chunks need only 3 minutes in total.
- **Make it faster:** Grate the garlic and ginger into the pan on a microplane instead of chopping.
- **Bulk it out:** Plain rice, or rice noodles dropped into the sauce for the last 2 minutes.
- **Leftovers:** Three days chilled. The turmeric oil sets on top as a bright orange cap — stir it back through as it reheats.
