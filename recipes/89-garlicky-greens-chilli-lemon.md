# 89 · Garlicky Greens with Chilli & Lemon

**Cuisine:** Greek · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 8 min (3 prep / 5 cook) · **Serves:** 4 · **Vegetarian**

> Horta, more or less: greens cooked hard and fast in good oil, then flooded with lemon the moment the pan comes off.

## Why it works
Sliced garlic started in cold oil warms through gently and turns sweet, where garlic dropped into an already hot pan scorches before the greens have even wilted. The lemon is held back until the heat is off, because acid added to a fierce pan cooks off and goes flat — thirty seconds of patience keeps it bright.

## Ingredients
- 500 g (1 lb 2 oz) cavolo nero, chard or spinach, thick stems sliced, leaves torn
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- ½ tsp dried chilli flakes
- 1 lemon, halved
- ½ tsp flaky sea salt

## Method
1. Put the oil and garlic into a large cold frying pan and set it over a medium heat. Let the garlic come up slowly for about 2 minutes, until it is barely gold at the edges and smells sweet rather than sharp.
2. Add the chilli flakes, then the sliced stems, and stir for 1 minute. Turn the heat to high.
3. Pile in the leaves with the rinsing water still clinging to them, add the salt, and turn them with tongs for 2 minutes until they have collapsed to a quarter of their volume and gone dark and glossy.
4. Take the pan off the heat, squeeze over both lemon halves and turn once more. This is the four minutes your chicken should be resting.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Kale, spring greens, chicory or beetroot tops. Tougher leaves want an extra minute with a lid on the pan.
- **Make it faster:** Bagged spinach needs no stem work and only 90 seconds in the pan, which brings the whole thing down to 5 minutes.
- **On the side:** Grilled fish with the skin crisped, roast lamb, or a plate of white beans and feta.
- **Leftovers:** Keeps 3 days chilled. Eat it cold with more lemon and oil, the way a taverna would serve it, or stir it through eggs.
