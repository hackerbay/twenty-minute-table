# 40 · Sambal Green Beans with Egg

**Cuisine:** Malaysian · **Method:** Wok · **Total time:** 12 min (4 prep / 8 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Beans blistered in a dry wok, then tossed through a sweet-sour sambal with soft-set egg clinging to them.

## Why it works
Dry-frying the beans before any oil goes in blisters the skins and collapses them just enough to take on sauce; a wet pan only steams them limp. The egg goes in at the end onto a hot, clear patch of wok, so it sets on contact and wraps the beans instead of scrambling into curds.

## Ingredients
- 600 g (1 lb 5 oz) green beans, topped and halved
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) groundnut or sunflower oil
- 4 shallots, finely sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp sambal oelek
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) tamarind paste, or the juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) honey
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) fish sauce
- 6 eggs, beaten with a pinch of salt
- 60 g (½ cup) unsalted roasted peanuts, roughly crushed
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Put the wok over your highest heat, dry. Add the beans and toss every 30 seconds for 3 minutes, until streaked brown and beginning to wrinkle. Tip them into a bowl.
2. Add 2 tbsp of the oil, then the shallots and garlic, and fry 90 seconds until golden and fragrant.
3. Stir in the sambal and fry 1 minute — it should deepen in colour and the oil separate slightly at the edges.
4. Add the tamarind, honey and fish sauce, then return the beans. Toss 1 minute, until every bean is coated and glossy.
5. Push everything aside, add the last tablespoon of oil to the clear space and pour in the eggs. Count to twenty while they set at the base, then fold the beans through in big slow strokes. Take the wok off the heat while the egg still looks wet — it carries on cooking.
6. Scatter with the crushed peanuts and serve with lime wedges.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Long beans, sugar snaps or tenderstem broccoli all dry-fry the same way. Cashews instead of peanuts.
- **Make it faster:** Beat the eggs in the bowl the beans are resting in and save a wash.
- **Bulk it out:** Rice, or fold 200 g of cooked rice noodles through at step 4.
- **Leftovers:** Two days chilled. Eat it at room temperature — reheating toughens the egg.
