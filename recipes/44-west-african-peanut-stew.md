# 44 · West African Peanut Stew Express

**Cuisine:** Ghanaian / Senegalese · **Method:** One pan · **Total time:** 20 min (6 prep / 14 cook) · **Serves:** 4

> Coarsely grated sweet potato melts into peanut butter and tomato in a quarter of the usual time.

## Why it works
Grating the sweet potato rather than cubing it takes the cooking time from forty minutes to eight — the shreds collapse and thicken the stew from the inside, doing the job a long simmer normally does. The peanut butter goes in late, loosened with a ladle of hot liquid first, because it splits and turns grainy if it catches on the base of the pan.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) groundnut or olive oil
- 500 g (1 lb 2 oz) boneless chicken thighs, cut into 2 cm (¾ in) pieces
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 20 g (1½ tbsp) fresh ginger, grated
- 1 scotch bonnet or red chilli, deseeded and chopped
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 400 g (14 oz) tin chopped tomatoes
- 400 ml (1⅔ cups) chicken stock or water
- 400 g (14 oz) sweet potato, coarsely grated, skin on
- 120 g (½ cup) smooth 100% peanut butter
- 200 g (7 oz) spinach
- 30 g (¼ cup) roasted peanuts, roughly chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a wide, deep pan over a high heat. Brown the chicken in one layer for 3 minutes without stirring, then turn once.
2. Add the onion, garlic, ginger, chilli and ground coriander. Cook 2 minutes, until the pan smells sharp and sweet at once.
3. Tip in the tomatoes, stock and sweet potato. Bring to the boil, then simmer briskly for 8 minutes. The shreds should vanish into the sauce and the liquid should coat a spoon.
4. Drop the heat to low. Stir the peanut butter into a ladleful of the hot liquid in a mug, then pour it back — it emulsifies smoothly instead of clumping.
5. Add the spinach in handfuls, stirring until just wilted, about 1 minute. Salt to taste.
6. Serve with the peanuts scattered over and lime wedges to squeeze.

## Chef's notes
- **Swap:** Two tins of drained chickpeas instead of chicken; add them at step 3.
- **Make it faster:** Grate the sweet potato in a food processor while the chicken browns.
- **Bulk it out:** Steamed rice, or fufu if you have it.
- **Leftovers:** Four days chilled, better on day two. Loosen with water when reheating.
