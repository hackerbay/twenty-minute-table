Pick My Meals
for a Week

Say how many dinners you want, how many you are feeding, and what the house eats. This picks that many out of the fifty lunches and dinners in the book, spreads the protein, the pan and the part of the world they come from, and adds every ingredient into one list in the order a shop is walked. Swap any night you do not fancy and the list follows.

A week is already picked below. Nothing is stored: the week you are looking at is in the address bar, so you can bookmark it or send it to whoever is going to the shop. Straight to the list

Dinners

Most weeks are not seven dinners. Pick the number you will actually cook. Picked from the fifty lunches and dinners; breakfasts, puddings and the sides stay where they are.

Serves

Every recipe in the book is written for four. Another number scales the shopping list — whole things and tins round up, since you cannot buy two thirds of a tin, and weights round to the nearest sensible figure. The recipe pages still read for four, so halve or double as you cook.

On the table

Untick anything nobody in the house eats. The numbers are how many dinners contain each thing, and they add up to more than fifty because some dishes are on two lists — a chicken stir-fry seasoned with fish sauce is chicken and fish, and unticking either one drops it. Anchovies melted into oil until they have vanished still count; eight of the fifty carry fish only as a seasoning. It is a filter on what the recipes list, not an allergen check — a jar of kimchi or a shop-bought curry paste can carry shrimp without the recipe saying so — so read the ingredients if it matters.

The pan

Half the dinners in the book are one pan and only four need no heat at all, so unticking is quick work — the counts say how much of the book each one is. Leave them all on unless the air fryer is in use or the hob is not.

50 of the 50 dinners match. Enough for five.

Picking again replaces every night. To change one night, swap it.

Nothing matches those ticks.

Every one of these ends with a note on feeding a toddler. Most lift a small portion out of the same pan before the salt and before the chilli; a few say plainly that theirs is easier cooked alongside. The note is at the foot of each recipe.

What is in this week

The figures are the estimates printed in each recipe, for a quarter of the finished dish. They describe the dinner alone — not what you put beside it, and not the rest of the day — and they are not dietary advice.

The shop

One list for the whole week, aisle by aisle, with the amounts added up and scaled to the number you are feeding. Ticks last while this page is open. Staples are at the foot — check the Fast Pantry before you buy them twice.

The first is the shopping list and then every recipe in full, laid out to print — about seven sheets of paper, and the week is on the fridge door. The second is the list on its own.
